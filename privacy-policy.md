# Privacy Policy — HideMyContact

**Last updated:** March 2026

## Overview

HideMyContact is a Chrome extension that blurs contact information (phone numbers and email addresses) displayed on [planity.com](https://planity.com). It is designed to protect the privacy of professionals listed on the platform.

## Data Collection

This extension **does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data** of any kind.

- No user data is sent to any server.
- No analytics or tracking are used.
- No cookies are created or read.
- The extension operates entirely locally within your browser.

## Permissions

The extension requests access to `planity.com` pages only, solely to detect and blur contact information displayed on those pages. No data from these pages is stored or transmitted.

## Third Parties

This extension does not integrate with any third-party services.
